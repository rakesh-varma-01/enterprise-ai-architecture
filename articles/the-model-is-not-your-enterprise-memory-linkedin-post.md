When an enterprise AI assistant gives a weak answer, replacing the model is often the most visible response—and the wrong first move.

The real problem may be missing ownership, stale sources, weak metadata, broken authorization, or context that lacks business meaning.

In this week's article, I explore why the model is not your enterprise memory, why more context is not always better, and what it takes to turn retrieval into trusted enterprise context.

One architecture question captures the issue:

Can the system explain why each piece of context was selected, why the user was allowed to receive it, how current it is, and who owns its meaning?

If not, the system may have retrieval—but it does not yet have trusted enterprise context.
